Our story

Then life sped up. Laptops, deadlines, a phone that never stops.

The kaapi got colder. The conversations got shorter.

Cafés rush you out. Offices close at six. Home is never quite quiet.

So many of us spend the day moving from one table to the next, never really settling.

We kept looking for a place that simply lets you stay.

Good coffee. A seat you can keep. Music that rises as the evening falls. People you start to recognise.

We couldn't find it. So we're building it.

Two friends, building it for home. One of us has been planning it from the US, missing exactly this. A warm room in Chennai to work in the morning, read in the afternoon, and stay when the record starts at night.

With love, the two of us behind Honey & Hum
